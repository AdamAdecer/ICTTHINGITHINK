A Beginner's Guide to Ping Pong

Table Tennis is essentially just tennis except it's on a table. It is more fast-paced than tennis due to it's smaller nature.

Find a table tennis center

  • A full table tennis setup is hard or maybe even impossible to fit into your house, so going to a table tennis center should be where you start.
  • In a center, you can practice your strokes and serves for as long as you want and maybe even be taught by a coach there

Have a buddy

  • Table tennis requires two people at the end of the day, so having a friend not only enables you to play with someone else more frequently, but it also can help you grow.
  • Sometimes, people can analyze you better than you can analyze yourself, as they aren't biased. Your friend can act as a coach, pointing out your mistakes and teaching you how to get better.

Practice, practice, practice

  • As mentioned in the description, table tennis, while smaller than regular tennis, still requires a lot of agility which can only be gained by training.
  • Although you will need to learn a lot of things in table tennis, you will need far, far more practice than knowledge, as with any other sport.
